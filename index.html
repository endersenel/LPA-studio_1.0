Methodology & Statistical Framework Report

This section details the formal statistical models, equations, and estimation algorithms utilized within the SPPLab studio, structured for thesis and journal manuscript integration.

1. Latent Profile Analysis (LPA) & Mixture Modeling

Latent Profile Analysis (LPA) models each observation as a finite mixture of multivariate normals. Full covariance matrices are projected to the SPD cone via Jacobi eigen-decomposition with eigenvalue clipping (ε = 1e-6 default).

f(y_i; Ψ) = ∑_{c=1}^K π_c φ(y_i; μ_c, Σ_c)

2. Model Selection & Enumeration Criteria

  • AIC / BIC / SABIC: Standard penalized-likelihood criteria for model comparison.
  • Entropy: Classification certainty metric; near 1 indicates precise assignment.
  • BLRT: Parametric bootstrap-based LRT with resilient replicate tracking (default B=100).

3. Validation & Group Comparisons

Exact ANOVA and Chi-Square engines use incomplete gamma/beta approximations for accurate p-values in small samples.

4. Joint Softmax Multinomial Logistic Regression & Pseudo-Inverse SE

Standard errors use an eigenvalue-based pseudo-inverse of the Fisher matrix; condition numbers > 1e12 raise flags and return 'FLAGGED SE' in output.
