Latent Profile Analysis

Statistical engine: Gaussian finite mixture model estimated by EM with multiple random starts. The default LPA covariance structure is VVI: class-specific variances and zero within-class covariances. This is appropriate for continuous indicators but should be chosen according to the theoretical/model specification of the study.
For most LPA applications, VVI or EEI is a defensible starting point. VVC estimates within-profile covariances and can become unstable with many indicators or small classes.
