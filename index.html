Exhaustive Profile Group Comparisons (ANOVA & Chi-Square)

Guidelines: After running LPA in Tab 2, evaluate auxiliary variables across all extracted profiles. Continuous variables output One-Way ANOVA with group-specific $n$, Means, $SD$, $F$, $df$, and $p$. Categorical variables output cross-tabulation frequencies ($n$), percentages ($\%$), Pearson $\chi^2$, $df$, $p$, and Cramer's $V$.
